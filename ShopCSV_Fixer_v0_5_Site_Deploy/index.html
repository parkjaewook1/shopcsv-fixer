PRIVACY FIRST

Your supplier CSV stays in your browser.

Files aren't sent to our servers. The tool contains no third-party analytics, no trackers, and no remote dependencies. Mapping profiles are stored in your browser only; product rows are not saved by the tool.

Read privacy and safety limits →
  • Draft, unpublished product status

  • Warnings and import-blocking checks
  • No Shopify credentials required

TRANSPARENT BETA

What it deliberately does not do

Not supported: product updates, multi-variant imports, multi-image galleries, stock sync, real URL availability checks, and direct store imports.

Shopify import step: Always review the export, disable overwrite, and import a few test products first. We cannot guarantee every supplier file will work.
